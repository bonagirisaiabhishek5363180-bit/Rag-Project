The Project

What is Mondroid?

Mondroid is an advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system. At its core, it is a bridge between static text repositories and intelligent synthesis, designed to parse complex documents and answer questions with high precision and citations.

The Problem

Information overload is a persistent challenge. While generative AI models are incredibly articulate, they suffer from hallucinations and lack context regarding private, specialized, or up-to-date data. Finding an exact answer buried in thousands of pages of documentation using standard keyword searches is inefficient and prone to error.

The Idea

The project was born out of the necessity to ground generative AI in reality. By combining semantic understanding with exact-match retrieval, the goal was to build a system that not only reads your documents but understands the nuance and context behind every paragraph, delivering answers rather than search results.

The Technology

Mondroid leverages a hybrid retrieval architecture. It uses ChromaDB for dense vector embeddings and BM25 for sparse keyword matching. These streams are merged and rescored using a CrossEncoder model, guaranteeing that the context provided to the final generative layer (powered by models like Gemini) is of the highest possible quality.
