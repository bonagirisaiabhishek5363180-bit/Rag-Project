# Google Flow Video Generation Prompts

To create the lag-free scrolling experience, we will use the **Continuous Walkthrough Architecture (Architecture A)**. This means there are no "dive-in" or "connector" clips, just one continuous forward flight divided into 5 legs (one for each section).

**Important Rules for Google Flow / Nano Banana Pro:**
- Render Leg 1 using the base image as the starting frame.
- Render Leg 2 using the *last frame of Leg 1* as its starting frame.
- Render Leg 3 using the *last frame of Leg 2* as its starting frame, and so on.
- Make sure there is no end-frame conditioning, only start-frame conditioning.
- **Duration:** Each video leg should be generated to be **8 to 10 seconds long**.

---

### Global Style Preamble (append this to the end of EVERY prompt)
`Ultra-photorealistic architectural photography of a grand circular concrete library interior, dark premium cinematic aesthetic, warm golden-hour rim lighting. Floating transparent glass screens and loose papers gracefully suspended in mid-air in the center. Sleek modern design, editorial magazine quality, shallow depth of field, centered composition with a little headroom. Absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no people.`
